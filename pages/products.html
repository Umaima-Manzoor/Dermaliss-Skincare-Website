Hydrating Mask

Benefits

  • Deeply hydrates and nourishes the skin while you sleep.
  • Helps improve skin texture, making it soft, smooth, and plump.
  • Soothes and calms the skin, reducing redness and irritation.
  • Leaves the skin looking refreshed and glowing in the morning.

Directions

  • After cleansing and toning your face, take a small amount of the mask.
  • Apply an even layer across your face, avoiding the eye and lip area.
  • Leave it on overnight and rinse off with water in the morning.
  • Use it 2-3 times a week as the final step of your nighttime skincare routine.

Ingredients

  • Hyaluronic Acid
  • Beta-Glucan
  • Ceramides
  • Sleep-Tox™ Technology

Healing Serum

Benefits

  • Deeply hydrates and plumps the skin..
  • Reduces redness and irritation.
  • Strengthens the skin barrier.

Directions

  • Cleanse your face and pat dry.
  • Apply 1-2 pumps of the serum to your face and neck.
  • Follow up with a moisturizer to lock in the hydration.
  • Frequency: Use twice daily (morning and night).

Ingredients

  • Hyaluronic Acid
  • Vitamin B5 (Panthenol)
  • Ceramides

Exfoliating Toner

Benefits

  • Exfoliates dead skin cells, revealing a brighter complexion.
  • Improves skin texture and reduces the appearance of pores.
  • Helps with uneven skin tone and hyperpigmentation.

Directions

  • Use after cleansing your face at night.
  • Soak a cotton pad with the toner and swipe it across your face, avoiding the eye area.
  • Follow with a hydrating serum or moisturizer.
  • Frequency: Use 2-3 times per week, especially if you have sensitive skin.

Ingredients

  • Glycolic Acid
  • Tasmanian Pepperberry Extract
  • Aloe Vera

Acne Treatment

Benefits

  • Reduces the size and redness of pimples quickly.
  • Prevents future breakouts by unclogging pores.
  • Helps fade post-acne marks over time.

Directions

  • Cleanse your face and dry it.
  • Apply a small amount of the treatment directly onto the affected area.
  • Follow with a moisturizer.
  • Frequency: Use once daily (nighttime recommended).

Ingredients

  • Benzoyl Peroxide
  • Niacinamide
  • Ceramides
  • Salicylic Acid

Lip Therapy

Benefits

  • Helps fade lip pigmentation and brighten the lips.

  • Leaves lips feeling soft, smooth, and plump.

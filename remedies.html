Honey Coffee Face Mask

    Benefits:
  • Exfoliation: Coffee grounds gently scrub away dead skin cells, revealing a brighter complexion.
  • Antioxidant Boost: Both coffee and honey are rich in antioxidants, which help combat free radicals and signs of aging.

    • Directions:

    1. Mix: In a small bowl, combine the ground coffee and honey until a smooth paste forms.
    2. Apply: Gently massage the mixture onto your face and neck, avoiding the eye area.
    3. Relax: Leave the mask on for 15-20 minutes.
    4. Rinse: Rinse off with lukewarm water and pat your face dry with a soft towel.

    Optional: For extra hydration, you can add a teaspoon of yogurt or coconut oil to the mask.

    Tip: Use freshly ground coffee for the best results.

    Enjoy your radiant skin!

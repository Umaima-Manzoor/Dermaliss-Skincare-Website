MASSAGES






GUASHA MASSAGE

  • Makes skin smooth and even
  • Improves facial contours and complexion
  • Reduces puffiness
  • Eliminates wrinkles
  1. Use the long side of the scraper too massage along the line from the nose to the ear
  2. Use the edge of the scraper to massage along the line starting from the corner of your eye
  3. Use the short side of the scraper to massage the nose bridge upward
  4. Use the long side of the scraper to massage the entire forehead upward
  5. Apply these steps to both side of the face




ROLLER MASSAGE

  • Helps to reduce facial puffiness
  • Improves complexion
  • Improves skin tone
  1. Use a large roller to massage the entire forehead upward
  2. Roll along the nose bridge to the hairline with a small roller
  3. Use a small roller to roll along the line just below your eyebrow
  4. Use a weak roller to roll along the line starting from the corner of your eye
  5. Open your mouth as if you're going to say "O" and use a small roller to roll up and down along the nasobladial fold
  6. Use a large roller to roll along the jawline upward
  7. Use a large roller to roll along the line starting from the corner of your lips upwards
  8. Repeat the steps for the other side of the face too
  9. After your massage session . Use your fingertips to gently tap over your forehead,cheeks,chin,and area around the eyes




SCULPTING MASSAGE

  • Tone, lift, and define facial muscles.
  • Enhance facial contours
  • Reduce puffiness, and improve circulation
  1. Place the sculptor at the center of your chin,Glide it along the jawline, moving from the center of your chin toward your ear
  2. Use upward strokes, glide the sculptor outward toward the temple.
  3. Starting in the center of the forehead, glide the tool upwards toward the hairline moving toward the temples on both sides.
  4. Use the edge of the sculptor , glide near the inner corner of the eye and move outward toward the temple.
  5. Place the sculptor at the base of your neck and glide upward toward the jawline. Repeat on both sides of the neck
  6. For the back of the neck,glide from the base of the neck upward toward the base of the skull.
  7. After your massage session . Use your fingertips to gently tap over your forehead,cheeks,chin,and area around the eyes

GUASHA MASSAGE

    Benefits:

  • Makes skin smooth and even
  • Improves facial contours and complexion
  • Reduces puffiness
  • Eliminates wrinkles

    Directions:

  1. Use the long side of the scraper too massage along the line from the nose to the ear
  2. Use the edge of the scraper to massage along the line starting from the corner of your eye
  3. Use the short side of the scraper to massage the nose bridge upward
  4. Use the long side of the scraper to massage the entire forehead upward
  5. Apply these steps to both side of the face




ROLLER MASSAGE

    Benefits:

  • Helps to reduce facial puffiness
  • Improves complexion
  • Improves skin tone

    Directions:

  1. Use a large roller to massage the entire forehead upward
  2. Roll along the nose bridge to the hairline with a small roller
  3. Use a small roller to roll along the line just below your eyebrow
  4. Use a weak roller to roll along the line starting from the corner of your eye
  5. Open your mouth saying "O" . Roll up and down along the nasobladial fold
  6. Use a large roller to roll along the jawline upward
  7. Use a large roller to roll along the line starting from the corner of your lips upwards
  8. Repeat the steps for the other side of the face too
  9. After your massage session . Use your fingertips to gently tap over your forehead,cheeks,chin,and area around the eyes

SCULPTING MASSAGE

    Benefits:

  • Tone, lift, and define facial muscles.
  • Enhance facial contours
  • Reduce puffiness, and improve circulation

    Directions:

  1. At the center of your chin, Glide the sculptor along the jawline,from your chin toward your ear
  2. Use upward strokes, glide the sculptor outward toward the temple.
  3. Starting in the center of the forehead, glide the tool upwards toward the hairline
  4. Use the edge of the sculptor , glide near the inner corner of the eye and move outward the temple.
  5. At the base of your neck glide the sculptor upward toward the jawline. Repeat on both sides of the neck
  6. For the back of the neck,glide from the base of the neck upward toward the base of the skull.
  7. After massage. Use your fingertips to gently tap over your forehead , cheeks and chin.

KANSA MASSAGE

    Benefits:

  • Improves skin tone and texture
  • Reduces swelling and puffiness.
  • Removes toxins, preventing acne and other skin issues.

    Directions:

  1. Using the Kansa wand,gentlly circulate on the forehead, cheeks, and jawline
  2. Start at the center of the forehead and move outward.
  3. Sweep under the eyes and along the jawline.
  4. Trace the sides of the nose down to the chin.
  5. On face and neck, use upward and outward strokes to lift and tone the skin.
  6. Ensure even coverage, spending a minute or two on each section.



ICE GLOBES

    Benefits:

  • Reduces Puffiness and Inflammation
  • Promotes Lymphatic Drainage
  • Relieves Muscle Tension

    Directions:

  1. Place the globes in the fridge or a bowl of ice water for about 15–30 minutes.
  2. Start at the sides of your nose and move outward toward your ears.
  3. Roll the globes from the inner corners of your under-eye area to the temples.
  4. Massage along your jawline, starting at the center of the chin and moving outward toward the ears.
  5. Glide the globes from your collarbone upwards toward your jawline, following the natural shape of your neck.
